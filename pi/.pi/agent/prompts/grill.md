Grill me on `${ARGUMENTS:-the idea I provide}` until we share a precise understanding. Model it as a decision tree and work in rounds, asking every currently unblocked question while deferring dependent ones. Number each question, explain the choice, and recommend an answer. Research facts yourself; ask me only for decisions. Recompute the tree after each round. When no questions remain, summarize the decisions and ask me to confirm our shared understanding.
